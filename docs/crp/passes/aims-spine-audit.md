# Specific Aims Audit Against the Locked Spine (2026-08-20)

Audit of `specific-aims.md` (Drive snapshot 2026-08-17, last Drive mod 2026-08-14)
against `SPINE.md` (locked 2026-08-20). Method: every claim tested against the
strategic test (does it move a family from identifying care to establishing it, or
prove that someone will pay for the machine that does?), the vocabulary register, the
temporal frame, and the evidence ledger. This is the delta list that feeds the
abstract and Aims-page rebuild. It contains no new prose.

## Verdict

**The three-aim architecture survives the spine test. No aim is added, deleted, or
reordered.** Aim 1 is the care-establishment capability on the family end; Aim 2 is
the other end of the connection (provider capacity and participation); Aim 3 is the
commercial hypothesis. The connection-completion measurement the spine requires slots
inside Aim 2 with a hook in Aim 1's opt-in flow; it is not a fourth aim. Every delta
below is framing, addition, or fact, not structure.

## A. Framing deltas (must fix in the rebuild)

1. **Background, sentence 3: the benefits-first move.** "$58 billion goes unclaimed"
   is the third sentence of the entire application, the inherited Phase IIB opening.
   Replace with the identification/establishment gap (families do not fail for lack
   of information; they fail between identifying appropriate care and establishing
   it; no existing source of help is accountable for establishment). $58B demotes to
   a supporting clause inside affordability.
2. **Background: agent overstatement.** "the agents screen families for eligibility
   and need, match them to financial aid and services, and carry out the
   administrative steps that establish care" states the execution capability in the
   present tense. Ledger flag: overstated. Correct to the temporal frame: matching
   and guidance operate today; the remaining Phase IIB year integrates the agent
   system; the CRP builds execution and follow-up. The Aims page must not promise
   less work than the award proposes to fund.
3. **Aim 1 rationale: benefits-first at aim level.** "Financial aid is one of the
   main reasons families come to Olera and the hardest one to act on" frames Aim 1
   as the benefits aim. Reframe as the establishment aim: families can identify aid
   and care today; the falloff is in establishing it; Aim 1 builds, verifies, and
   validates the establishment capability. Aid remains the featured instrument (it
   is genuinely the hardest step and the differentiator), one level down.
4. **Aim 2: product count and naming.** "three unmet needs: staffing, client
   acquisition, and marketing" (and RS Aim 2's four modules) conflict with the
   ratified two-product framing: Managed Ads and Staff Recruitment. Rebuild states
   two products; whether "Growth Suite" survives as an umbrella name is an open
   question (strategic-context §6) to settle with TJ during the rebuild.
5. **Aim 2: the missing endpoint.** The spine requires "concentration... until care
   connections complete reliably." Current Aim 2 measures delivered tool value
   (applicants, hires, inquiries, admissions) but never commits to measuring whether
   family-provider connections complete: provider response rate, time to response,
   care established from consented referrals. Add connection completion as a
   first-class Aim 2 measurement and result clause. This is the twice-agreed handoff
   experiment surfacing at Aims level.
6. **Objective: the free core is absent.** The spine makes "families pay nothing and
   connections remain free" load-bearing; the Objective never says it. Add once, in
   the Objective, so Aim 3's "keeps the platform free for families" lands as a
   design principle rather than a slogan.
7. **Market count: provisional, not vague.** "twelve local markets" (Objective,
   Aims 1 and 2, "the thirteenth market") is downstream of the Approach redesign.
   Do not soften to "selected markets" on the final Aims page (an Aims page needs
   specifics); tag every occurrence [PROVISIONAL: market count pending Approach
   redesign] and lock the number when the Approach pass sets it.
8. **Vision paragraph: rewrite from the spine's closing.** Current text is one
   run-on with vague abstractions ("resource-connected population", "basic
   life-saving services"). The spine's final sentence is the replacement raw
   material: independently verified model, provider revenue covering family cost to
   serve, investable, scalable, free for families.
