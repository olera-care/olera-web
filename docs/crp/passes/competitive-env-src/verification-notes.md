were marked "remove before proposal insertion" in the draft, so they are kept
here rather than rendered into the section.

- Eldercare Locator is a national information-and-referral service; BenefitsCheckUp
  screens benefit eligibility and directs users toward application resources.

  financial-support work, applications, advocacy, and provider coordination.

  new-workforce advantage should remain framed as a CRP hypothesis to be tested.
- General-purpose AI and human-navigation ratings are necessarily category-level
  and variable; individual implementations may exceed the representative rating.

## Formatting decisions made in the house-style pass

Prose is the author's, unchanged. Ratings are the author's, unchanged.

1. **Rating marks.** The draft used filled, half-filled, and em dash. The
   half-filled circle (U+25D0) sits outside Arial's core glyph set, so it falls
   back to another face and lands at a visibly different weight beside the other
   marks. Replaced with a size progression that is entirely core Arial: large dot
   (core capability), small dot (partial or variable), ring (not typical). This
   also removes the em dash, which house style does not permit.
2. **Short column headers.** "Adds new workforce" and "Open family and provider
   access" were shortened to "New workforce" and "Open access", with both
   definitions moved into the caption. At full length they starved the five
   pathway columns and Word broke their headers mid-word.
3. **Four advantages as a 2x2 block** rather than run-in prose inside one
   paragraph, matching the advantages block in Commercialization Plan Section 4.
4. **Olera row shaded** so the comparison row is findable without reading.

## Open questions for Logan

- **Column names do not match the canonical pathway.** The Commercialization Plan
  uses a seven-step care-establishment pathway (Assess Needs, Identify Care, Fund
  Care, Staff Care, Execute Plan, Establish Care, Track Outcomes). This table uses
  Assess, Find, Fund, Execute, Establish plus New workforce. "Find" is "Identify
  Care" and "New workforce" is narrower than "Staff Care". Aligning the labels is
  part of the homogenization pass rather than a formatting change, so nothing was
  renamed here.
- **Track Outcomes is missing as a column**, and it is arguably the strongest
  differentiator: every competitor in the table would take a ring. Worth adding.
- **The two-mark cells** ("core / partial" for human and digital navigation) are
  preserved as drawn. Since "partial or variable" already covers variability, a
  single mark would read more cleanly, but that would change a rating.
