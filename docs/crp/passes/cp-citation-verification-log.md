## Not verified

**Reference 1, AHRQ Technical Brief No. 49.** The document's 52% / 34% and
36% / 24% figures could not be confirmed against the brief. Four retrieval
attempts (AHRQ effectivehealthcare site, NCBI Bookshelf NBK615046, PMC10736117,
PMC10737203) returned the brief's framing but not those specific percentages.
The citation to the brief itself is correct for the general claim it supports;
the two percentage pairs should be checked against the printed brief, or dropped,
before submission. This is the one open citation in the document.

## Corrections made

1. **BLS reference years.** The document said 2024 employment and 2024 to 2034
   projections. The current handbook reports 2025 employment and 2025 to 2035
   projections. Both the prose and the reference entry now say 2025 and
   2025 to 2035.
2. **"Five peer-reviewed studies" to "Four."** The repository holds four
   peer-reviewed papers; the fifth item is the unpublished CARE-NAV TAS study.
   The claim now says four, which is what the evidence supports.
3. **GUIDE model goals.** The document attributed a broader set of goals to
   GUIDE than CMS states. Trimmed to the confirmed language about delaying
   nursing-home placement, alongside the verified PBPM, navigation, and
   caregiver-support elements.
4. **Table 7 markers.** The table still carried the revenue-stream section's
   local numbering (`benchmark1,3` and `benchmark2`). Renumbered to 6 and 14.
5. **Section 1 placeholders resolved.** `[refs]` to 1, `[refs]` to 2 and 3,
   `[cite]` to 4.

## Judgment call for the team

Reference 7's 63.3% (providers turning down cases in 2023) has been superseded:
the 2025 Activated Insights report puts the 2024 figure at 39%. The document
cites both editions, so a reviewer who knows the series may notice that the older
and larger number is the one carried in the prose. Options are to update to 39%
(weaker number, current), to keep 63.3% with the year stated as it already is
(accurate as written, since the sentence says 2023), or to state both. Left as
is, with the year explicit, pending your call.
