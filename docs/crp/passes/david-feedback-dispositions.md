## Feedback 1.1 (2026-08-25 email, on the logos document)

- **Logistics question ("are we still doing a one-pager?").** Answer to David:
  yes; the logos document is internal scaffolding, not a submission artifact.
  The package remains Aims 1p / RS 12p / CP 12p.
- **Jargon comment.** Mostly moot once internal-vs-application vocabulary is
  explained: "empty quadrant," "intellectual spine," "sunk science," "causal
  chain" were never destined for application prose. Gentle correction owed:
  "central hypothesis" and pre-committed "alternative approaches" are standard
  NIH language reviewers expect, not startup jargon.
- **FFS incentive argument (new, and the one item that engages H5).** His claim:
  fee-for-service providers lack incentive and resources to invest in
  innovation; risk-bearing entities are the natural payer. Rebuttal for
  Thursday: our products do not ask providers to invest in outcomes or clinical
  decision support; they redirect spend providers already make on client
  acquisition and staffing (incumbents prove the category spend, thousands per
  client). Different purchase psychology; his Town Square analogy does not
  transfer. His point strengthens the CP second horizon rather than weakening
  H5.
- **North Star / clinical outcomes (ER visits, 30-day readmissions,
  adherence).** Declined as endpoints: needs claims data, comparison groups,
  and time the CRP does not have; would reweight human subjects mid-sprint.
  Establishment stays the North Star as the proximal measurable step. ADOPT his
  packaging: the "outcome / economic value / replicability" triad as the
  award-end proof structure for the CP's Value-outcomes-impact section
  (outcome = H1 establishment rate; economic value = H2/H5/H6; replicability =
  playbook + county map). Optional middle path parked with Qiping: pre-registered
  secondary self-reported utilization measures in the Aim 1 IRB study.
- **Flywheel.** His flywheel is the causal chain with a payer loop appended.
  ADOPT: CP presents the causal chain as a flywheel (investor-native language,
  zero substantive change). One sentence each in the CP for the telemetry/data
  asset (sharpens existing competitive-advantage claim) and self-insured
  employers as a later segment (Homethrive as comp).
- **Net sprint impact:** nothing in 1.1 touches Aims or RS; all of it lands in
  the CP pass and the Thursday call. RS and CP feedback from David still to come.
