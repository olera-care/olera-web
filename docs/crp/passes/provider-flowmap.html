The plausibility check, honestly stated

The gate combines three things per product: (1) value shown — the product reached its value endpoint at credible rates in both streams; (2) providers want it — continued use and stated willingness to continue; (3) the arithmetic is plausible — stated willingness-to-pay, discounted by a pre-registered haircut (stated WTP routinely overstates real payment), minus measured cost to deliver, clears the bar — with acquisition cost from the Aim 2 outreach channels included, because a product can carry a delivery margin and still cost too much to sell.

What it cannot establish: actual conversion, actual retention, actual revenue — Aim 3's job, and the proposal should say so in one sentence. On the 20% threshold: it appears nowhere in the current Research Strategy or Commercialization Plan — the existing concepts are the derived sustainability threshold (measured under Aim 3 and CPA-verified), 3:1 LTV:CAC, and 12-month payback; the CP's per-account model implies a ~60% contribution margin. Recommendation: derive the gate's bar from the CP sustainability model at reconciliation, don't inherit a round number. bar TBD
