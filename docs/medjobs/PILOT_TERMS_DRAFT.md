## What this is

Olera runs the Student Caregiver Program: we find pre-health students — pre-med,
pre-nursing, pre-PA — who want paid, hands-on caregiving work near their
university, and we introduce them to providers who need caregivers.

This is a pilot. It is written to be read once and kept. There is nothing to
sign and nothing to pay to start.

## What we do

- We send you students who match what you have told us you are looking for:
  hours, shift types, certifications, and anything else you tell us matters.
- Each student arrives as a single page — what they are studying, when they can
  work, what they have done before, and a short video.
- We reach you by email and by text, and every student is waiting in your
  portal. Some students will ring your office directly.
- We keep sending students until you hire one and the placement works out.

## What you do

- You decide who to interview and who to hire. We do not place anybody with
  you; we introduce them.
- You employ the people you hire, on your own terms, at your own rates, under
  your own policies.
- You tell us when you hire somebody who came through us, and how it is going.

## What we are not

We are not an employer of record, a staffing agency or a licensing body. We do
not employ the students, set their pay, supervise their work or guarantee their
performance. Hiring, screening to your own standard, background checks where
your state requires them, supervision, payroll and insurance remain yours.

## The pilot, and what it costs

Nothing, until it works.

- The pilot runs until you hire your first student through us **and** that
  placement works out.
- There is no obligation to continue at any point, and no notice to give. Tell
  us to stop and we stop.
- **If you want to carry on after that**, our standard terms are **$250 per
  hire**. We would agree those with you then, in writing, rather than now.

> **Open:** whether the first hire is free, and whether a hire is billed when
> it is confirmed or at the end of the month, are not settled. See C1. The
> paragraph above says *after the first successful hire* because that is what
> Logan described; it is not yet a decision.

## Your students' information

A student's page is shared with you so you can decide whether to interview
them. Use it for that. Do not pass it on outside your organisation.

## Stopping

Email the person who sent you this, or reply to any message from us. We will
stop sending students the same day. Anybody you have already hired is yours;
nothing here follows them.
